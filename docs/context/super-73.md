- **Command Center**: Overview, Calls, Insights, Safety, Keith's Brain, Test Lab. Used by MAC, Taylor and testers. State: deployed 4 Nov with placeholder data (Super73 <> Supernal, 3 Nov 2026; Notion).
- **Pulse**: Supernal's client portal showing the org chart, milestones and tasks; Super73 was one of the first clients on the new version (Super73 <> Supernal, 20 Oct 2026; Product Standup, 6 Oct 2026).

**Feature rationale**

1. Front door for all calls, but only troubleshooting resolved at first: do one thing well, and it is the highest-volume, hardest call type (Super73 <> Supernal, 29 Sep and 6 Oct 2026).
2. Safety issues (thermal event, cracked frame, injury) transfer at once with no AI handling (Discovery Call, 25 Sep 2026; Super73 <> Supernal, 6 Oct 2026).
3. Explicit coded rules per workflow, not documents fed to the model: the client has detailed SOPs and wants predictable behaviour (Super73 Internal Alignment, 17 Sep 2026; Vitor / Diana Internal Sync, 13 Oct 2026).
4. A fast, cheap backend model that calls a retrieval tool: smarter models were too slow, and latency is the parity measure (Super73 Internal Sync, 6 Nov 2026).
5. Do not transfer on the first request; try a few steps, check the caller is with the bike, reassure early (Super73 <> Supernal, 13 Oct 2026).
6. No personal details needed for troubleshooting; three pieces verified before order changes (Super73 <> Supernal, 29 Sep and 10 Nov 2026).
7. Zendesk read-only during early testing, with test tickets tagged into a controlled queue (Super73 <> Supernal, 13 Oct and 10 Nov 2026).
8. Go direct to a carrier (Twilio or Telnyx), because Zendesk's telephony gives no direct API access (Super73 <> Supernal, 20 Oct 2026).
9. When no transfer is possible, create a ticket in the right group, not a Slack post; Keith stays on 24 hours (Super73 <> Supernal, 10 Nov 2026).
