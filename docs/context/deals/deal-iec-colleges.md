## File: Concept-Alignment

## 01 · The Problem
Leads cost more and arrive in smaller numbers. Google's AI-overview shift took organic search from 23% of enrolment to under 14% (Drive: IEC Background), and cost per lead rose about 5x while conversion held (Weekly Pipeline Meeting, 2 Sep 2026). Tuition is capped and costs rise 7% to 10% a year, so acquisition is the one lever left (Drive: IEC Background). Underneath, IEC has no shared library of what it has published, what each page claims, and what compliance has already approved. As a result, search, social and compliance all run by hand and go unmeasured (Notion: IEC Colleges Strategy).

## 02 · Proposed Approach
IEC Marketing OS sits downstream of IEC's marketing team and agencies, connected read-only to existing tools. Phase 1 (months 1 to 3) proves leads on UEI's 22 campuses through a weekly boost list, a website claims library, search fixes in Rich's sprint, and one compliance queue with scored, sourced drafts. Later phases add paid landing pages, then Sage, then US Colleges (Drive: IEC Colleges - Order Form - V 11.12).

## 03 · Outcomes We're Targeting
More leads from organic search, more social leads at a known cost, and lower paid cost per lead from month 4. Esther's goal is 1 to 2 extra starts per campus per month (Drive: IEC Colleges - Order Form - V 11.12). She has said 20 to 40 incremental starts a month across 22 campuses (IEC / Supernal Check In, 27 Oct 2026).

## 04 · How We'll Know It Worked
Targets with a 12-month baseline are signed off in month 1. Measures: social leads and cost per lead per boosted post (month 1), search rankings and clicks (months 1 to 2), time from draft to compliance approval (month 2), and organic leads by page (months 4 to 6). Applications and starts are tracked but not committed (Drive: IEC Colleges - Order Form - V 11.12).

## 05 · Next Steps

### 2. The client and their problem
IEC runs about 56 campuses in 17 states with 2,200+ staff, and about 80% of its revenue comes from federal student aid. Campuses have been full for two years. Tuition cannot rise while costs climb 7% to 10% a year, so the only open lever is acquisition. That lever is breaking. SEO drove 23% of enrolment and now drives under 14%, falling about two points a month as Google moves to AI overviews. IEC spends $1.2M to $1.4M a month on Google Ads it cannot cut, and cost per lead is up about 5x while conversion stays strong. Inside marketing, a team of about eight serves every campus with no designer. It handles about 50 compliance visits a year, where one program change can touch 50 or more files. It runs 23 Facebook and 23 Instagram pages for UEI alone, and builds reports by hand from Salesforce, Power BI, Funnel and Excel. No one can find every place a claim appears.
