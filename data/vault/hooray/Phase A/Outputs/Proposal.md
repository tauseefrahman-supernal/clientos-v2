## HOUSE RULES (do not violate)
- **No em dashes.** No hours or points. No open-ended deliverables ("improve", "support", "assist" without a defined output).
- Lead with what the client has **already built**, then the gap we extend.
- Name their **#1 objection** and answer it directly.
- Show a concrete **first 30 days**. 3 outcomes max in the overview, not a long deliverables list.
- Include a business-case number (cost of the status quo, not just the upside).
- A "how we work together" block: who owns what, how they stay in control.
- Every "what we heard" quote pairs a pain with a worry, so it reads honest.
- Commercials and next steps must be consistent (same tiers, same numbers).
- CTA is action-specific (sign here, pick a tier, schedule kickoff), never vague.

## Section structure (mirror the HTML reference)

### Overview
_(Title, subtitle. North star: their goal + the business-case number. What they have already built. What we extend.)_

### The Problem
_(Carried from discovery, hardened with scoping evidence.)_

### Landscape
_(What SaaS exists and what it covers; where a custom build is justified.)_

### How We Work Together
_(Embedded partner, not a vendor. Who owns what; how the client stays in control.)_

### The Build / The OS
_(Phase 0 / Phase 1 / Phase 2 narrative. Milestone-based. Fully operational in 90 days framing where it fits.)_

### What Comes Next
_(Committed direction beyond the first phases, re-scoped as we learn.)_

### Roadmap
_(Milestones + timeline. Month-1 visible win, Month-3 end state.)_

### Commercials
_(Tier / monthly fee. Same numbers as the order form. No points, no hours.)_

### Next Steps
_(Action-specific CTA: sign MSA + first order form, confirm named owners, set kickoff.)_
