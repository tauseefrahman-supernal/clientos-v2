## Call 1 — Discovery I (Apr 30, 2026)

**Format:** Two sessions — a short pre-call (10:59 EDT, ~10 min) followed by the main call (11:58 EDT, ~45 min)
**Participants:** David Metze, Roger Burns, Nick Agresti

### Main call — what we learned

**The ask (initial framing):**
David's stated goal: replace client-facing account manager roles with AI agents — specifically for scheduling, communications, campaign ideation, and creative brief creation. He envisioned an agent connecting to internal systems, client CRMs (Revenate, Sendine), Google Analytics, and Meta.

_→ Surfaced to A2 Layer 1 (stated problem) and A3 Layer 2 (objectives)._

**What Supernal confirmed as doable:**

**Architecture discussion:**
David disclosed: had tried OpenAI agent builder and was considering Google Vertex. Nick positioned Supernal's proprietary orchestration platform as the right foundation — modular, stable, continuously improved. David's 60-day build philosophy addressed by Nick's counter: build for the long term, refine as tech advances.

_→ Surfaced to A3 Layer 5 (operating context, tech stack) and A2 Layer 3 (operational problem — stalled chatbot)._

**Pricing model introduced:**
Pod structure (~$10k/month for team of four + maintenance per module). David's payback frame: replacing three employees. Roger's cleaner frame: natural attrition as new accounts onboard. Nick reframed: reimagining the org, not replacing people.

_→ Surfaced to A5 (engagement model) and A3 Layer 1 (client — business model)._

**Proof of performance concern named:**
Randy, Steven, Shelly will want evidence. Nick proposed: 30-minute YouTube video (two client testimonials) + direct client reference call.

_→ Surfaced to A2 Layer 4 (organisational problem — proof-of-performance wall) and A4 (Concierge — closing trust move)._

**Hospitality track record confirmed:**
Supernal has built for a boutique hotel (custom front desk agent) and a restaurant group.
