## Intake Summary

Hooray Agency is a ~30-year-old (founded 1993, rebranded 2019), Irvine CA–based independent luxury-hospitality marketing agency with approximately 22–36 employees and ~$7.6M in reported revenue. It manages dozens of hotel/resort clients (Salamander Collection, Resorts World Las Vegas, Curator Hotel & Resort Collection, Aqua Aston, Marcus Hotels, Preferred Hotels and many others), running paid media, creative, web, social, and SEO/AEO services. A lean, senior-loaded team carries a high account load — creating exactly the conditions under which a reporting copilot creates leverage.

The agency's account team (account executives + directors) is operationally constrained: an estimated 60%+ of account team time is consumed by low-value coordination — deciphering vague client briefs, back-and-forth email clarification, and manual monthly performance reporting. The agency has attempted to solve this internally (OpenAI chatbot on RAG database, then a stalled Google Vertex migration) but the dev team keeps being pulled to client-facing fires.

The Supernal opportunity has two phases:
- **Phase 1:** SAL as a Reporting Copilot — data aggregation from core ad platforms → narrative summaries → anomaly detection → AM-ready packages.
- **Phase 2:** SAL as a full intake-to-package intelligence layer — client request intake, classification, clarification, creative brief packaging into Monday.com.

The political challenge is significant: Randy Stuck (EVP) and the internal dev team will resist external spend. The deal strategy is build-on-spec / pay-over-time + Hooray retains process IP + client reference connections for Randy and Steven. Internal champion path runs through Steven Seghers (influencer), then Randy (EVP approver), then Shelly Weasel (emotional/technical buyer).

**Pricing lens (from Compass research):** At $5k/month, SAL is priced below a single mid-market client retainer ($8k–$25k/month), cheaper than a junior analyst's fully-loaded monthly cost (~$5,800–$7,900/month), and far below true competing platforms (NinjaCat/Improvado ~$36k–$120k+/year). The risk is Shelly or Randy anchoring against a $69–$799/month SaaS dashboard (Swydo/AgencyAnalytics). The proposal must pre-empt this objection explicitly.

**Comparable Supernal engagement (OVG Order Form reference):** Supernal's OVG/CVR One engagement is a $4,000/month × 12-month = $48,000 platform build (Cover One news intelligence platform). Comparable complexity: single platform, milestone-based V1, post-V1 rolling monthly cycles, client owns codebase from day one. Confirms Supernal's standard commercial and delivery model for a comparable project scope. Hooray's engagement ($5k/month MSA + order forms, Phase 1 + Phase 2) is structured similarly but larger in scope and includes two Work Products across two phases.

The SAL Vision Deck (file 196) is the most complete Supernal-authored articulation of the engagement to date. It formalises the agent name, ROI model, competitive framing, outstanding questions, and the seven-step closing sequence. It is the de facto proposal foundation document for the June 10 feedback pass.
