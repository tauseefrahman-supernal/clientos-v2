## Stated Ask

> "Help us solve an internal agency problem — not a hotel problem."

In their words (Discovery II, David Metze): account team members spend 60%+ of their time on work that shouldn't require them — deciphering vague client briefs, endless email clarification loops, and manually pulling and narrating monthly performance reports. They want an AI employee that handles these tasks so the same account team can carry more hotel clients at a higher strategic level.

**Phase 1 (the wedge):** Reporting copilot. SAL pulls data from GA4, Google Ads, Meta Ads, (Trade Desk TBC), writes first-draft performance narratives, surfaces anomalies and next steps — ready for an AM to finalise in 10 minutes rather than build from scratch.

**Phase 2 (the vision):** Full intake-to-package intelligence. SAL receives client creative requests, classifies and clarifies them via structured dialogue, and packages a complete creative brief into Monday.com for traffic assignment.

The umbrella is **SAL** — an AI employee with four responsibilities: **intake → classify → clarify → package.**
