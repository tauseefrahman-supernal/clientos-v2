## What each goal means for a strategist's accounts

Stella should check every account against these questions. Each answer is something Stella can verify or draft.

1. **Baseline (Value and retention).** Does the account have a written baseline and 2 to 3 client-specific metrics? If not, it misses the Nov 30 bar several strats set and the company "every account has a baseline" goal. Stella drafts the metric set from the account's KPIs and the order form's Milestone 1 KPIs.
2. **Monthly value review.** Is there a monthly value presentation (MOR or MBR style) on the calendar and in the cadence? Account OS cadence kind: MBR. If the last business review is over 100 days old, health is At risk at best (see Strat-Playbook.md).
3. **At-risk ARR.** Is this account part of the at-risk ARR the team must bring under $500K? Any account in Watch/At risk or Critical needs a recovery path with an owner and a date.
4. **Expansion plan.** Is there a documented expansion plan with a trigger (date or metric)? The plan should be priced on the current offering (see Offering-and-Commercials.md). A pod added to the account counts toward the "4 accounts add a pod" target.
5. **Margin.** Is the account's margin status known (Profitable, Break even, Losing money) and recorded in the shared system? New work must price at 45% or higher. Flag any expansion that lowers the account below 45%.
6. **Strat load.** Does the strategist carry 3 to 4 accounts or $400K to $1M managed revenue? Stella can compute this from Account OS ARR and capacity rows.
7. **Case study and referral.** Is a case study secured or in progress, and has the client been asked for a referral? Both roll up to company goal 3.
8. **Systems.** Can someone else open the account and understand its past months in minutes? Transcripts, decks, demos and metrics should be linked from the account, and the weekly report should be current.
9. **One GTM system.** Did any new proposal or order form on this account use the standard offer, pricing and contract process? Off-standard deals need a stated reason.
