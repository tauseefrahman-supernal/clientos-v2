## HOUSE RULES
- Phasing is driven by **milestones**, not by unit counts. A milestone is a dated, client-legible moment — "portal live for pilot users", "first renewal brief sent" — not "4 units delivered".
- Every milestone needs a month, an owner, and an observable signal that it happened.
- Name the engagement tier and its monthly fee. Tier changes the **pace** milestones arrive at, not the milestone list.

## Phase Transitions

A phase is "done" when its milestones have landed with their observable signals, the deliverables are producing Keystone entries at or above the projected volume, AND their Pulse metrics are within agreed thresholds. Care owns the "are we meeting thresholds?" call. Concierge owns the "are we ready to start the next phase?" conversation with the client.
