# AI Strategy Roadmap — [Client]

> **Phase:** Delivery Strategy · **Role:** Input
> **Answers:** The strategy deliverable of the 12-month partnership, built in parallel with the first build. It maps the client's path to AI-native, prioritises what we build next, and by Month 3 shows leadership a concrete pipeline that makes continuing the obvious choice.
> **House rules:** Lead with department + use case + the metric we're accountable for. Scope Phase 1 to <= 3 months with a visible Month-1 win. Everything beyond Month 6 is a rolling plan. No em dashes.
> **Framework:** `Frameworks/5-Layer-Context-Model.md`

## 1. Business Context
- **Client profile:** industry, business type, headcount across teams we touch, what they do, how they generate value (where the margin comes from)
- **Snapshot:** what they do, size, teams involved
- **Business goals (12-18 months):** revenue, cost, speed targets AI must serve
- **Why now:** what triggered this; what the exec sponsor cares about most
- **Definition of success:** the one sentence the sponsor would use at renewal
- **Industry research:** market & trends, competitive landscape, regulatory / data constraints (cite sources)
- **Our POV:** where AI moves the needle most here; what we'd avoid; the non-obvious bet; biggest risk if nothing changes

## 2. Current State
- **Workflow map:** Department · Key workflow · Pain point · Time / cost today · Owner
- **Systems & data inventory:** System · Used for · Access status (requested / granted) · Data quality · Integration notes
- **AI maturity:** what they've tried before and what happened; team skills and appetite (champions and skeptics by department)
